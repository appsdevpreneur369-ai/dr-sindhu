Google doesn't ask for any documents to create the listing. 
Verification is done by video, phone or email. 
Documents only come in if Google asks for extra proof, which happens more often for new businesses and during suspension appeals. 
So collect these upfront and keep them ready:

Needed on day one (not documents, but mandatory)

Gmail ID owned by the clinic or doctor, to be the Primary Owner
Permanent signboard showing the exact clinic name you'll use on Google
Clinic phone number that she answers, for verification by call or SMS
Full address with landmark and PIN, plus the Google Maps pin
10+ photos: exterior with signboard, reception, treatment room, doctor, equipment

Keep ready in case Google asks for proof (any 1–2 usually work):

Trade licence / municipal licence from Mangalagiri–Tadepalli Municipal Corporation, in the clinic's name
Clinic registration under AP's Allopathic Private Medical Care Establishments (APMCE) Act, which covers private clinics, dental included
Utility bill (electricity or internet) at the clinic address, ideally in the clinic's or doctor's name
Rent agreement or property document for the clinic premises
GST certificate, if registered; it's not mandatory for small clinics
Shops & Establishments registration, if taken
Business bank account statement or cancelled cheque in the clinic's name

Useful for the profile, not for Google verification

Doctor's registration: Dental Council of India / AP State Dental Council number and BDS/MDS certificate. Good to show on the website and in the clinic.
Clinic logo: high resolution, square version for the profile picture.

Tips for a 2-week-old clinic

Names must match: the signboard, utility bill and licence should show the same clinic name as the Google profile. Mismatched names are the most common reason Google rejects verification.
If the trade licence or clinic registration is still being processed, the application receipt plus a utility bill or rent agreement at that address is usually enough.
Never upload Aadhaar, PAN or bank details into the verification video. Show them only if Google specifically asks for a document, through its official upload form.

I haven't checked the exact names of these AP registrations and the local municipality against official sources. Please confirm them with the clinic; she may already have some of them from setting up.
