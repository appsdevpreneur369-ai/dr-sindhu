## What this website stores

This website does **not** use advertising or analytics cookies.

It uses your browser's **session storage** (which is cleared when you close the tab) for two small things:

- remembering that the booking pop-up has already been shown, so it does not appear again during your visit
- keeping your booking details while you move between the booking steps

## Third-party services

Some pages include content from other services, which may set their own cookies when you interact with them:

- **Google Maps** — the map on the Contact page
- **WhatsApp** — only when you click a WhatsApp button
- **ClinicFlow247** — when you follow a link to sign in, register or view the clinic's booking page

Their own cookie and privacy policies apply.

## Managing storage

You can clear cookies and site data at any time in your browser settings. The website will still work; the booking pop-up may simply appear again.
