The information on this website — including treatment descriptions, patient education articles and FAQs — is **general information**. It is not a diagnosis and is not a substitute for an examination and advice from a qualified dentist or doctor.

- Every mouth is different. The treatment, number of visits and results described here are typical and may not apply to you.
- Words such as "usually", "may help" or "linked with" are used deliberately: they describe what is common or what research suggests, not a promise.
- Do not delay seeking care, or stop a prescribed medicine, because of something you read here.
- **In an emergency** — such as swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, uncontrolled bleeding or a serious injury — go to the nearest hospital emergency department or call 108.
