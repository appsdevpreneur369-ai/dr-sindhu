Deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) removes hardened deposits from the root surfaces below the gum line. It gives inflamed gums the chance to heal and tighten around the teeth.

## What you may notice

- **Sensitivity to cold** for a week or two. Roots that were covered in tartar are now clean and exposed to temperature changes.
- **Gums look slightly smaller.** As swelling goes down, the gum line can sit a little lower. This is the gum becoming healthier, not the cleaning "removing" gum.
- **Light bleeding** when brushing for the first few days.
- **Small gaps between teeth** where swollen gum used to fill the space.

## Looking after your gums

1. Use a **soft toothbrush** and brush gently along the gum line twice a day.
2. **Clean between your teeth** daily with interdental brushes or floss, as shown at the clinic.
3. A **sensitive-teeth toothpaste** can ease the cold twinge; give it one to two weeks.
4. Use any mouthwash only if it was prescribed, and for as long as advised.
5. **Avoid smoking** — it slows gum healing noticeably.

## The review visit

Your periodontist will usually see you again after four to eight weeks to re-measure the gum pockets. Many pockets shrink after deep cleaning alone. Deeper pockets that remain may need further treatment, and a regular maintenance interval will be agreed to keep the gums stable.
